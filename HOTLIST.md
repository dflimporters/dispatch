# HOT list

Agreed ideas that are not scheduled yet. Pull one into a phase when it's ready.

- **Barcode scanning** at the pick face (camera or Bluetooth scanner). Every item already has a barcode in Acumatica.
- **Pick accuracy** per area and per picker: shorts, overs, checker corrections.
- **Short-picks trigger cycle counts**: Acumatica said 20, the picker found 12.
- **Live load board**: picking %, checked, trucker, loaded, invoiced per load.
- **Load-out photo** at staging, attached to the load.
- **Timestamps on every step**, to see where time goes (pick, check, trucker).
- **Weight alongside volume** in batching, once the weight data is reliable.
- **Drop sequencing / reverse-order loading.** Not used today; truckers load as they like.
- **Driver app with proof of delivery.**
- **Real AI help (later)**: unusual-load and unusual-order flags, reading rep notes, short-pick summaries for purchasing, rep order entry.
- **Switch the area mapping to Acumatica Warehouse Locations** after the warehouse move.
